Helina Bezabih is an Associate at TBeST Law LLP with experience in corporate, commercial, and investment law. She has supported complex due diligence projects, advised on regulatory compliance, and assisted both local and international clients across diverse sectors. Before joining TBeST, she worked as an in-house legal counsel and junior consultant at Adwa Partners Consultancy, a management Consulting Firm. Helina holds an LLB from Bahir Dar University and is currently pursuing her LLM at Addis Ababa University.
Selected Experience

Assisted on legal due diligence reviews for large-scale projects, including assessments of corporate, regulatory, and contractual compliance.
Advised on merger filings and competition law compliance for cross-border transactions.
Drafted and reviewed commercial contracts, including distribution, lease, and service agreements.
Supported multinational clients on investment and regulatory matters, including foreign exchange, licensing, and export compliance.
Provided legal research and analysis on fintech, digital services, and emerging areas of law such as electronic signatures.
