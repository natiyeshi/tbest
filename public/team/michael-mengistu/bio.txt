       PROFILE
	Michael Mengistu is a senior Associate at TBeST Law. He earned his LL.B. with great distinction from Addis Ababa University School of Law and holds an LL.M. from the University of Groningen.

	Before joining TbeST Law, Michael worked at the Ethiopian Investment Commission within the Policy Research Directoraye and the Industrial Parks Facilitation Directorate. He also practiced as an Associate at Mesfin tafesse & Associates Law Office.

	SELECTED RELEVANT EXPERIENCE

Advised domestic and international companies on corporate governance matters, including company restructuring, mergers, and acquisitions.

Drafted and reviewed a wide range of contracts for corporations and non-governmental organizations including shareholders’ agreement.

Provided legal advice on contract law, foreign exchange regulations, and investment laws in Ethiopia.
