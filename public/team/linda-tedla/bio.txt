Linda is an Associate at TBeST Law LLP and serves as the lead associate in the firm’s Intellectual Property practice. She has earned her Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) from Mekelle University and is currently pursuing her LLM in Business and Property Law at Addis Ababa University. With two years of experience in the IP field, her work focuses on trademark and patent prosecution, including preparing and filing applications before the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Authority, managing opposition and renewal processes, advising on enforcement strategies and other IP needs of clients. Linda also supports IP due diligence and broader corporate and commercial work. Before joining TBeST, Linda has experience as a volunteer in the IDP department at the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission and worked as an Administrative/Event Intern at the GIZ Ethiopia & Djibouti Country Office, where she assisted with contracts, events, ticketing, and services for national and international staff.
